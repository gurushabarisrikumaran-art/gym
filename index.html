Training Philosophy

"You cannot out-train a bad diet, and you cannot out-recover poor sleep. Mastery is finding the balance between intensity and recovery."

Strength First

Progressive overload is the core. If the bar isn't getting heavier, or the reps aren't increasing, you're just exercising, not training.

Optimal Recovery

Muscle is built outside the gym. Strict sleep schedules, mobility work, and active recovery are non-negotiable.

Mind Muscle Connection

It's not just about moving weight from A to B. It's about feeling the targeted muscle contract under maximum tension.
